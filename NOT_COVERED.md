# Coverage gaps and limitations

- **Unbuilt requirements.** The dynamic alternating click latch, separate endpoints for two scales on one ruler, general Part viewport section plane, light theme, arbitrary STEP protocol selection, general preferences and a full ISO catalog have no corresponding release implementation. The inventory distinguishes these from confirmed defects.
- **Design baselines.** No preapproved complex-part mass/volume or process baseline was supplied. New FreeCAD measurements and hashes are candidates. Geometric agreement between two OCCT kernels does not certify a design. Analytic controls, STEPcode, mesh readers and actual section measurements provide distinct evidence.
- **Blocked projects.** The M10 × 0.75 pen variant and modeled M5 × 12 library screw could not generate valid solids. Those exact cases have no positive downstream manufactured file to check. Alternative-size pen, scratch modeled M5 pair and cosmetic library fallback are recorded separately.
- **Mechanisms.** The sampled and continuous travel checks cover the documented driven joint and pairs. They do not establish physical spring dynamics, wear, friction, toleranced minimum separation or latch retention on a machined pen. Joint hierarchy/appearance are not transferable as editable Turnmill constraints. Placement failure is ASM-001.
- **UI and human factors.** The new-user simulation used Chromium and no manual. Human timing, accessibility testing with assistive technology, real devices, all OS keyboard layouts, every shortcut in every context, and an exhaustive tooltip census remain open. Studio Measure supplies mass and balance at the relevant Part steps; drawing/assembly screens have other readouts. Screenshots and geometry were taken at modeling milestones rather than after every pointer movement.
- **Paper and manufacturing.** PDFs were parsed and rendered; no physical paper/laser/CNC run, caliper or ISO thread gauge was available. External material/color fidelity is outside the current geometry exchange paths. Detailed independent checks cover the tested references/format directions, not every import entity a commercial CAD file can contain. PDF has no model reimport path, and 3MF has no native import button.
- **Automation bounds.** Strict seeded fuzz uses valid rectangular extrude/revolve edits, undo, redo and reload. Arbitrary finishes/cam-chain generation is outside its domain. Directed tests cover other features. Timeouts bound each isolated solver test; this is not a global proof a solver can never hang. Local numerical/kernel results were obtained on macOS arm64 in Chromium, FreeCAD 1.1.4 and the listed independent readers. Performance depends on hardware, competing workloads and browser rendering.
- **Release stage.** Phase 1 did not publish a live site or change product behavior. The configured Pages source is GitHub Actions. GitHub-hosted and locally executed results are tracked separately in the report; a scheduled one-hour run is not counted as an observed pass merely because a schedule exists.
