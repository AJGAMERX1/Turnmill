# Comparison of Turnmill and FreeCAD geometry

Each of the nine visual-review JPEGs shows actual screenshots. Columns show the Turnmill viewport and FreeCAD's isometric, front, and enlarged views. Each row represents the same physical file or the same native geometry before export. Shape names appear above the first image. The four independent FreeCAD fixtures were also imported through Turnmill's **Import CAD file** dialog. Whole pen views can obscure the refill and clip. Their individual rows and per-solid measurements record those parts separately.

| Contact sheet | Observations |
|---|---|
| 1 | The 20 × 10 × 5 block remains rectangular in both views. The extruded stock with the failed rotated engraving shows the same shallow recess running off an edge. The cylinder appears circular from its tilted end. Both hinge leaves show an open bore and a flange extending in opposite directions. |
| 2 | The hinge pin extends past the separations between the leaves. Cosmetic thread lines visible on the library screw in Turnmill disappear from its exported smooth shank, consistent with their display-only mode. The modeled nut has visible internal grooves in both apps. The ruler blank has no tick cuts. |
| 3 | The engraved ruler shows two bands of marks. The analytic rod's threaded and smooth halves are visually distinct. The barrel's enlarged view shows its grooved shoulder and open bore. The separate cap's entrance is visible when viewing from the opposite end. The refill narrows to a tip. The degenerate STL facet in INTEROP-002 is smaller than this display scale. |
| 4 | The clip retains its angled attachment and narrow free end. In the home-coordinate model both end sections on the assembled twist pen remain visible. The library bolt/nut combination places the nut through the head in the exported coordinates; the separate nut and shank rows expose both geometries. |
| 5 | The scratch bolt has modeled grooves at its tip. Its nut shows the internal helix. Their combined view shows the nut coincident with the head, consistent with the measured positive overlap. The click study's guide and ring show circular open ends. |
| 6 | The click study's individual plunger and guide appear as concentric axial components in the combined model. The UI hinge's opposing flanges and its separate cylindrical pin both survive STEP exchange. |
| 7 | The independent UI hinge leaves have the same offset along the pin. The customized barrel carries narrow longitudinal cuts on its left-hand band and a shoulder thread at its right end. The cap entrance, tapered refill, and clip shape remain identifiable in separate views. |
| 8 | The assembled customized pen shows the added external marks. The UI ruler has two graduation rows. A native FreeCAD filleted block kept its rounded edges when imported into Turnmill. The FreeCAD straight-knurled cylinder and helical rod retain their grooves. |
| 9 | The spacer hole and the gap to the stop block are visible in both views. Their source labels are lost on return import, as recorded in INTEROP-004. |

Turnmill's brass, steel and aluminum display colors differ from the uniform grey in FreeCAD's imported views. Source material-colored appearance was not transferred. The visual comparison samples exterior views and cannot rule out hidden face defects. Console warnings from the macOS GUI launch concerned the unavailable optional 3Dconnexion controller and Python binding deprecations. Per-file FreeCADCmd imports and B-rep checks are logged separately. The standalone M5/CAD fixture is a nominal flank shape; the workflow does not certify tool selection, production tolerances or print strength.

The dedicated [shoulder](ui-pen-twist-pen-1.freecad-detail.png), [engraving](ui-pen-twist-pen-1.freecad-engraving.png) and [knurl](ui-pen-twist-pen-1.freecad-knurl.png) close-ups show the uninterrupted external helix, the actual letter outlines spelling ANA and the longitudinal flats cut into the cylindrical grip. On the actual UI ruler, the enlarged [FreeCAD graduations](ui-ruler-graduated-ruler.freecad-graduations.png) show the cuts and 0, 10 and 20 outlines. I read the exported metric number row from 0 to 150 in tens and the lower row from 0 to 6 in inches on both [UI](ui-scale.exported-numerals.png) and [parameterized](ruler.exported-numerals.png) SVG files. Both produce 23 outline text groups. The machine measurements verify every tick length, start, interval and both edges' counts and isolate the terminal-position error in INTEROP-005.
