# Visual inspection of exported PDFs

Reviewed the Poppler rasterization of all eight full pages in rendered-review.jpg, alongside the preview screenshots from the production UI. Barrel sheet 1 has three orthographic projections, an isometric view, an axial cut and the thread callout. The thread flank and bore are visible in the enlarged detail. Sheets 3 and 4 carry two parts of the dimension table without intruding on the title block. The repeated short outside-diameter stations describe successive interruptions by the modeled helix; this makes the table dense.

The assembly set shows seated views, a hatched section, four exploded bodies with individual balloons and a four-item BOM. The BOM and title blocks remain inside the borders. The contact sheet is reduced for comparison. See the vector PDFs for text at print size. All eight pages measure 297 × 210 mm. PDF text bounds, extracted numbers and minimum glyph sizes are recorded in pdf-measurements.json; no hardcopy was printed.

Also reviewed eleven exported pages from the actual finished and guided UI projects, for 19 total A4 pages. In rendered-review-2.jpg, the engraved ANA name and short straight knurl grooves appear on the finished barrel views and the dimension table lists their respective feature callouts. The finished exploded assembly shows item balloons for all four parts; its BOM remains on its own page. No clipping at page boundaries was visible in these examples.
