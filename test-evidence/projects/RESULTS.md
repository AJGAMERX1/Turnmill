# Real projects and personas

These scenarios ran in Playwright against a locally served production build with the read-only geometry hook. Each test begins in a fresh browser context. Project creation, typed sketches, constraints, feature changes, materials, joint settings and file downloads use visible UI controls. Exact geometry and saved poses come from the hook. JSON files alongside each image include the active measurements and console messages. Model timing includes the time spent recording evidence.

## Visual review and measurement scope

- **Hinge, clicker and coil.** The hinge screenshots show the moving leaf upright at 90° and alongside the fixed leaf at 180°. The left and right knuckles remain coaxial around the pin. The cam screen shows a follower and closing spring on the profile. In the physical spring insertion frame the silver coil initially projects across the tube. After the recorded rotation and placement it is enclosed by the housing. Internal clearance and hidden parts were checked numerically.
- **Ruler and pen.** The upper metric labels reach 150; the lower labels read 0 through 6. The 3D part shows two engraved edges. After the pen cuts, short axial lines appear in the grip band and the inscription appears farther down the barrel. At the broken M10 setting the model area empties, a rebuild error identifies the barrel thread and the mass readout clears. Eight grooves and engraved outlines are small at whole-model zoom.
- **Screws.** In the from-scratch model the uncut cylinder is round in the first frame. After machining, visible flanks extend over half the shank. In the library workaround cosmetic lines show the screw thread. The preserved failure frames show the connected-body diagnostic in both the viewport and the manual finishing dialog.
- **Drawings.** The part preview locates the section below the exterior views. The detail page shows two separated hatched threaded walls and a dimension at the bottom. The dimension page shows a table with a separate title block. The exploded page shows thin leader lines to the four separated bodies and a separate BOM. Individual screenshots retain full resolution.
- **Interactions.** The power-user series shows the renamed refill moving one row before the threaded cap and then returning and moving forward again. In chaos evidence the pre- and post-orbit view directions differ, with the same part and exact model state. The losing tab's status bar shows the local-save conflict and the surviving tab shows 99 mm after reload.
- Across the completed scenarios the captured console warnings were Chromium software-WebGL readback stalls. Browser page exception count was zero. Physical properties in Part expose mass and X balance; completed solid checkpoints compared both against the read-only hook within display rounding. Assembly and drawing screens do not expose the Part mass control. Assembly runs use the drive, spring and clearance readouts together with the hook; drawing runs inspect sheet geometry and export. Snapshots are taken at modeling milestones, not per individual vertex keystroke. Inspection of complex shapes is provisional until reference approval. See the main interoperability table for independent measurements.

Run from the repository with the QA production preview active: `node tests/projects/run.mjs`. Set `TURNMILL_URL` and optionally `CHROMIUM_PATH` to override the default browser and URL. Each named case can be replayed separately; `click-coil` consumes the native project created by `click-pen`, and `drawing` uses the enhanced pen when present. The full command runs producers before consumers.

Independent reader follow-up found that the terminal metric outline is centered 0.0375 mm inside its nominal position (INTEROP-005). The tested 0–150 mm metric and 0–6 in spans passed at interior scale positions. See [the per-file table](../interop/results.md).
